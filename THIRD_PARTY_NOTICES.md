# Third-party notices policy

WeftMark is licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Third-party material incorporated into or distributed with WeftMark remains subject to its own license and attribution requirements.

## Policy

- Prefer dependencies and reusable source with licenses compatible with Apache-2.0 and the intended open-source distribution of WeftMark.
- Preserve applicable copyright, patent, trademark, attribution, and license notices from third-party source.
- When code derived from an Apache-2.0 project is distributed in modified form, retain the Apache-2.0 license requirements, identify modified files where required, and carry forward any applicable upstream `NOTICE` attribution.
- Record substantial copied or adapted source, vendored assets, generated code with attribution requirements, and other notice-bearing material in this document or an accompanying `NOTICE` file before redistribution.
- Dependencies consumed only through normal package-management mechanisms retain their own licenses; their license metadata should remain available through the dependency inventory/SBOM rather than being represented as WeftMark-owned code.
- Do not remove upstream attribution simply because an integration is technically separable or because the upstream project uses the same license.

## Current state

No substantial Cline Kanban source is incorporated into WeftMark at the time this policy is introduced. The planned Kanban integration may study or later adapt Apache-2.0-licensed upstream code. Any such reuse must be recorded here, together with applicable upstream copyright and `NOTICE` information, before redistribution.
