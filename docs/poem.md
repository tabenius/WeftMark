# The Weft and the Delta

*A short poem about WeftMark, programming as creative mathematics,
and language as a computing machine.*

---

The warp is Git—the given and the true.
The weft is choice, the thread we throw across.
A *mark* records the reason we construe:
what changed, what proves it, and who signed the loss.

For code is mathematics dreamed awake,
a lattice deemed correct because it means;
and language is a machine we make
compile our sentences to running scenes.

Think Akka: actors sealed and small,
who share no secret, hold no lock—
they pass their messages down the hall,
and answer, patient, to the knock.

So water flows: it never forces stone,
it finds the fall and follows what is least;
and where the land goes flat, the river's thrown
to braided channels meandering east.

The delta's silt is *ledger*—every bend
records the water's argument with clay:
here it was allowed, here lineage penned,
the map of why the current shifted way.

WeftMark is that: the delta given form,
the mailbox for a fleet of writing hands,
the backpressure that keeps the merge from storm—
a woven proof of small, deliberate strands.
