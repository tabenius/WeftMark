# The Weft and the Delta

*A poem about WeftMark, programming as creative mathematics,
and language as a computing machine.*

---

On the loom of the machine we string the warp,
taut and vertical, the given and the true—
then throw the shuttle sideways, weaving sharp
across the fixed a thread that we construe.

The weft is choice. The warp is what is granted.
Between the two, a fabric learns to hold.
A *mark* is where the reason stands recanted
from noise to record: why the change was bold,

what altered, and what evidence attests,
who read the diff, and whether it is ready.
This is arithmetic the heart invests—
a proof that keeps a hundred authors steady.

For programming is mathematics dreamed:
not sums alone, but forms we choose to make,
a lattice where the beautiful is deemed
correct because it means, and cannot break.

We write in language, and the language *runs*.
The sentence is a lever and a gate;
each verb a state-machine that turns and turns,
each noun a register we allocate.

Grammar is a computing engine, quiet,
compiling thought to consequence and back—
the syntax of a river in its riot,
the parser of the current's chosen track.

Think Akka: little actors, sealed and small,
who never share a secret, never lock,
but pass their messages across the hall
and answer, mailbox-patient, to the knock.

No shared mutation—only sent intent.
A stream that pulls just what it can absorb,
backpressure teaching hunger to relent,
the fast made gentle by the slow's rebuke.

So water flows: it does not force the stone,
it finds the fall and follows what is least.
A million droplets, each one on its own,
compose a single will toward the east.

And where the land goes flat the river bends—
the delta braids, remembers, splits, and tries
a dozen channels toward its many ends,
depositing the silt of compromise.

That silt is *ledger*. Every meander
records the water's argument with clay:
here it was allowed, here it will wander,
here is the lineage of yesterday.

WeftMark is that: the delta given form,
the actor-mailbox for the fleet of hands,
the backpressure that keeps the merge from storm,
the map of why the river shifted lands.

So let the many agents write at once,
their warp of Git, their weft of what they mean.
The mark will hold the reason to the runts
of doubt, and keep the fabric wide and clean—

a mathematics made of care and current,
a language that computes and also sings,
a river disciplined but never deterrent,
a woven proof of small, deliberate things.
